# PulseVibe

PulseVibe is a GPU-accelerated ambient sound synthesizer and audio visualizer for mobile devices.

## Core Features

- **Generative Sound Engine**: Custom C++ audio graph running at 48kHz sub-millisecond latency.
- **Metal / Vulkan Shaders**: Reactive fluid dynamics shaders responding to live mic or synth audio.
- **Custom Oscillators**: Craft pink noise, binaural beats, and rain frequency profiles.
