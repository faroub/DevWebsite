# DukkanPro — Point of Sale & Store Management Engine

DukkanPro (دكان برو) is an offline-first POS and business management solution designed for retail stores, mini-markets, clothing shops, and micro-entrepreneurs.

## Key Features

- **Point-of-Sale (POS) Checkout**: Ultra-fast barcode scanning, custom item addition, thermal receipt printing over Bluetooth or USB, and automatic discount handling.
- **Customer Credit & Debt Tracker (*الكناش*)**: Full record-keeping for customer debt balances, partial payments, automated WhatsApp/SMS reminders, and statement history.
- **Inventory & Stock Control**: Live stock counts, low-stock warnings, cost vs. retail price analytics, and multi-category product organization.
- **Financial & Net Profit Analytics**: Real-time dashboards calculating daily sales, gross margin, net profit, and operational expenses.
- **Offline-First SQLite Engine**: Complete data autonomy — all transactions are stored locally with optional encrypted cloud backup.

## User Manual & Quick Start Guide

### 1. Setting Up Your Store & Products
1. Open DukkanPro and tap **Inventory > Add Product**.
2. Scan the item barcode using your phone camera or connected USB/Bluetooth scanner.
3. Enter the cost price, retail selling price, and initial stock quantity.

### 2. Processing a Sale & Printing Receipts
1. From the POS screen, scan product barcodes or tap product shortcuts.
2. Select the payment method (Cash, Card, or Credit Debt).
3. Tap **Complete Sale** — if a Bluetooth thermal printer is paired, receipts print instantly.

### 3. Managing Customer Debts (*الكناش*)
1. When selling on credit, select or create the customer profile.
2. The transaction is logged under the customer debt ledger automatically.
3. View debt balances, accept partial repayments, and issue digital receipts at any time.
